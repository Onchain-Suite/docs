---
title: "Allowlist and Claim Reminders"
description: "Remind allowlisted wallets to mint or claim before the window closes, and stop once they do."
---

**Goal.** You have a set of wallets eligible to mint or claim. Remind them in-app (and by email where you have one) before the window closes, and stop reminding each wallet the moment it claims.

**Who it's for.** Web3 teams running a mint, an allowlist, or a claim window. This is the wallet-native cousin of a Web2 [abandoned cart](/use-cases/web2/abandoned-cart): a started-but-unfinished action you want to recover.

**Outcome to move.** Claim / mint completion rate.

## Prerequisites

- The [in-app SDK](/integrations/in-app-notifications) installed, so reminders land in your dApp.
- Your mint/claim contract in [project settings](/getting-started/set-up-project-settings).
- The list of eligible wallets, imported or built as a [segment](/audience/segments).

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the eligible segment">
    [Import](/audience/imports-and-exports) the allowlist, or build the eligible cohort as a segment. This is who you'll remind.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remind before the window closes">
    Run an automation that sends an in-app reminder (and an email where available) on a schedule as the deadline approaches, using the [Date reached](/automation/triggers-and-conditions#off-chain-triggers) trigger:

    `trigger (Date reached: 3 days before close) → send in-app (Your mint window is open) → branch (has email → also send email)`

    Add a second reminder for the final hours.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stop reminding wallets that claim">
    Watch the contract with the **Token acquired** on-chain trigger, and when a wallet mints, tag it **Claimed**. Exclude **Claimed** wallets from the reminder segment so nobody who already minted gets nagged. See [Contract-based flows](/automation/contract-based-flows).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    Both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Tagging.** Tag wallets `claimed` with an **Add tag** action and exclude them from the reminder. See [Tags, lists, and segments](/integrations/tags-lists-segments).
</Note>

## Test

Add a wallet you control to the eligible segment, confirm the reminder arrives, then mint with it and confirm it drops out of the reminders.

## Measure

- **Claim rate** among reminded wallets versus a holdout.
- **Reminder-to-claim time**.
- **Email capture**: how many eligible wallets shared an email along the way.

## Variations

- **Tiered reminders.** A whale on the allowlist might warrant a more personal touch.
- **Post-claim welcome.** Chain a claim into [Welcome a new wallet](/use-cases/web3/welcome-wallet).
