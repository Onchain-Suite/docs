---
title: "Governance Vote Reminders"
description: "A proposal goes live, remind token holders to vote, and nudge the ones who haven't before it closes."
---

**Goal.** When a governance proposal opens, reach your token holders in-app and by email, then remind the ones who haven't voted as the deadline nears, to lift turnout.

**Who it's for.** Web3 protocols and DAOs with on-chain governance. Web3-only by nature.

**Outcome to move.** Voter turnout.

## Prerequisites

- Your governance contract in [project settings](/getting-started/set-up-project-settings).
- The [in-app SDK](/integrations/in-app-notifications) installed.

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger when a proposal opens (no code)">
    Use the **Governance activity** on-chain preset, which fires on a new proposal. The platform watches your contract for you. See [Contract-based flows](/automation/contract-based-flows).

    `trigger (Governance activity) → send in-app (New proposal: have your say) → branch (has email → also send email)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Remind the wallets that haven't voted">
    As the deadline approaches, re-reach holders who haven't cast a vote. The **Governance activity** preset also recognizes a vote cast, so tag wallets **Voted** when they do, and target the reminder at holders who aren't tagged **Voted**.

    `trigger (Date reached: 1 day before close) → audience = holders NOT tagged Voted → send in-app + email (Voting closes tomorrow)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Thank voters (optional)">
    A short "thanks for voting" keeps participation feeling rewarding and lifts turnout next time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    Both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test

With a wallet you control, confirm the proposal-opened message fires after the short finality delay, cast a vote, and confirm it's tagged **Voted** and excluded from the reminder.

## Measure

- **Turnout** per proposal, before and after reminders.
- **Reminder lift**: votes cast after the "closes tomorrow" nudge.
- **Channel mix**: in-app versus email participation.

## Variations

- **Delegate reminders.** Target wallets that hold voting power but usually delegate.
- **Quorum watch.** If a proposal is short of quorum near the deadline, send a stronger push.
