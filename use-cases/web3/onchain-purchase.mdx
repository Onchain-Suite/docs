---
title: "On-Chain Purchase Follow-Up"
description: "When a wallet buys, mints, or swaps, follow up automatically from the on-chain event, in-app and by email where you have one."
---

**Goal.** When a wallet completes a purchase on-chain, a mint, a swap, a deposit, follow up automatically: confirm in-app, suggest the next step, and email where you have an address.

**Who it's for.** Web3 teams. This is the wallet-native parallel of the Web2 [Purchase confirmation](/use-cases/web2/purchase-confirmation): the purchase happens **on-chain**, so there's often nothing to send from your backend, the platform sees it for you, and the first touch is **in-app** rather than email.

**Outcome to move.** Repeat on-chain activity and the share of buyers who take the next step.

## Prerequisites

- Your contract configured in [project settings](/getting-started/set-up-project-settings), so the platform can watch it.
- The [in-app SDK](/integrations/in-app-notifications) installed, so the follow-up can land in your dApp.

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger on the on-chain purchase (no code)">
    For EVM contracts, the platform watches the chain for you. In the [automation builder](/automation/create-an-automation), pick the preset that matches the purchase, **Swap completed**, **Token acquired** (a mint), or **Liquidity added** (a deposit), and give it your contract. That's the whole setup. See [Contract-based flows](/automation/contract-based-flows).

    `trigger (Swap completed) → send in-app (Thanks for your swap)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow up in-app, and by email where you have one">
    Add a **Send in-app** block, then branch so wallets with an email also get one:

    `trigger → send in-app → branch (has email → send email | else → in-app only)`

    The in-app message reaches the wallet in your dApp; the email reaches the ones who've shared an address through a [capture form](/audience/capture-forms).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Purchases your backend knows about (optional)">
    Some purchases aren't a plain on-chain event, a fiat on-ramp, a Solana contract, or an off-chain store tied to a wallet. Send those in yourself as a custom event keyed by the wallet:

    <CodeGroup>
    ```bash curl
    curl -X POST https://api.onchainsuite.com/api/v1/events \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \

        "event": "purchase_completed",
        "contact": { "walletAddress": "0xabc..." },
        "payload": { "sku": "pass-gold", "total_usd": 49.0 },
        "idempotencyKey": "purchase:0xabc:9184"
      }'
    ```

    ```python Python
    import os, requests

    requests.post(
        "https://api.onchainsuite.com/api/v1/events",
        headers={"Authorization": f"Bearer {os.environ['ONCHAIN_SECRET_KEY']}"},
        json={
            "event": "purchase_completed",
            "contact": {"walletAddress": "0xabc..."},
            "payload": {"sku": "pass-gold", "total_usd": 49.0},
            "idempotencyKey": "purchase:0xabc:9184",
        },
    ).raise_for_status()
    ```

    As with any automation, both are required.
  </Step>

## Test

Do the on-chain action with a wallet you control and wait out the short finality delay, then confirm the automation registered an entry and the in-app message arrived. See [Testing a flow](/automation/contract-based-flows#testing-a-flow).

## Measure

- **In-app delivered and viewed**, and email open and click where you sent one.
- **Repeat purchases** from wallets that entered the follow-up.
- **Email capture rate** among buyers who had no address.

## Variations

- **Upsell the holders.** Trigger on **Token acquired** and point minters at your next drop.
- **Win-back.** Pair with an [on-chain trigger](/automation/triggers-and-conditions) like **Exchange outflow** to catch a wallet heading for the door.
- **Web2 parallel.** A regular, off-chain checkout? Use [Purchase confirmation](/use-cases/web2/purchase-confirmation).
