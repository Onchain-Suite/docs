---
title: "Win Back Dormant Wallets"
description: "Catch wallets heading for the door, tokens to an exchange, liquidity withdrawn, or gone quiet, and pull them back in-app and by email."
---

**Goal.** Notice when a wallet shows an exit signal, moving tokens to an exchange, withdrawing liquidity, or simply going dormant, and reach them with a timely win-back before they're gone for good.

**Who it's for.** Web3 teams. This is the wallet-native parallel of the Web2 [Win back quiet subscribers](/use-cases/web2/win-back): instead of "stopped opening emails," the signal is **on-chain behavior**, and the first touch is **in-app**.

**Outcome to move.** Retained wallets, and recovered TVL or activity.

## Prerequisites

- Your contract configured in [project settings](/getting-started/set-up-project-settings).
- The [in-app SDK](/integrations/in-app-notifications) installed, so the win-back can land.

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger on an exit signal (no code)">
    For EVM contracts, the platform watches the chain for you. In the [automation builder](/automation/create-an-automation), pick the on-chain preset that matches your risk signal, **Exchange outflow** (tokens heading to a CEX), **Capital withdrawn** (liquidity pulled), or **Unstaked**, and give it your contract. See [Contract-based flows](/automation/contract-based-flows).

    `trigger (Exchange outflow) → send in-app (Everything okay?)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach them in-app, and by email where you have one">
    Lead with an in-app message (it reaches the wallet right away), then branch to email for wallets that have shared an address:

    `trigger → send in-app → branch (has email → send email | else → in-app only)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Also catch the quietly dormant">
    Some wallets don't signal, they just stop. Build a dormant cohort with a [SQL segment](/intelligence/reports-and-segments#save-as-a-segment):

    ```sql
    SELECT wallet_address
    FROM contact_360
    WHERE days_since_last_activity > 30
      AND portfolio_value_usd > 1000
    ```

    Run a separate automation off that segment to re-engage before they drift further.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    As with any automation, both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test

Perform the exit action with a wallet you control (or add it to the dormant segment), wait out the short finality delay for on-chain triggers, and confirm the automation registered an entry and the in-app message arrived. See [Testing a flow](/automation/contract-based-flows#testing-a-flow).

## Measure

- **In-app delivered and viewed**, and email engagement where sent.
- **Retention**: did flagged wallets stay active, or return on-chain, after the win-back?
- **Recovered value**: activity or TVL that came back versus a holdout.

## Variations

- **Tier the response.** A whale heading for the exit may warrant a different message, or a human, than a small wallet.
- **Pair with lending risk.** Combine with the [Health factor crossed](/automation/triggers-and-conditions#off-chain-triggers) trigger to catch borrowers before liquidation.
- **Web2 parallel.** Working from email engagement instead of on-chain signals? See [Win back quiet subscribers](/use-cases/web2/win-back).
