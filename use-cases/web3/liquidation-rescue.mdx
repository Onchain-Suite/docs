---
title: "Liquidation Rescue"
description: "Warn a borrower in time, when their lending position's health factor falls toward liquidation."
---

**Goal.** When a wallet's lending position drops toward the liquidation line, reach the borrower in-app and by email while they can still act, add collateral or repay, so they don't get liquidated.

**Who it's for.** Web3 lending protocols and teams whose users hold borrow positions. Web3-only by nature, and one of the highest-value alerts you can send.

**Outcome to move.** Liquidations avoided, and the trust that comes from catching them.

## Prerequisites

- The [in-app SDK](/integrations/in-app-notifications) installed.
- The lending pool and chain you want to watch (you'll set these on the trigger).

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Trigger when the health factor crosses your line (no code)">
    Use the **Health factor crossed** trigger. You set the chain, the lending pool, and the threshold, where `1.0` is liquidation, so a value like `1.2` warns the borrower early. See [Triggers and conditions](/automation/triggers-and-conditions#off-chain-triggers).

    `trigger (Health factor crossed 1.2) → send in-app (Your position is at risk) → branch (has email → also send email)`

    Lead with in-app: it's immediate, and a borrower checking their position is likely in your dApp.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escalate as it worsens">
    Add a second, more urgent trigger at a tighter threshold (say `1.05`) for a stronger "act now" message, so the urgency matches the risk.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm when they recover (optional)">
    A short "you're back in the safe zone" once their health factor recovers closes the loop and builds trust.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    Both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Test

With a wallet you control on a testnet pool, move the position toward the threshold and confirm the alert fires in-app and by email.

<Note>
This is a time-sensitive, high-stakes alert. Keep the message short, specific, and actionable, exactly what's at risk and the one thing to do about it, and test the timing carefully before going live.
</Note>

## Measure

- **Liquidations avoided**: positions that recovered after an alert.
- **Alert-to-action time**.
- **Opt-in**: how many borrowers share an email so you can reach them off-app too.

## Variations

- **Tier by position size.** A large position at risk may warrant a more personal touch.
- **Pair with win-back.** Combine with [Win back dormant wallets](/use-cases/web3/win-back) for borrowers who go quiet after a close call.
