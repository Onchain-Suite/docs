---
title: "Failed Payment Recovery"
description: "A charge fails, recover it with a short, well-timed dunning series before the account lapses."
---

**Goal.** When a subscription payment fails, recover it quickly with a clear, polite series, so a passive failure (an expired card) doesn't quietly become a churned customer.

**Who it's for.** Web2 SaaS and subscription businesses.

**Outcome to move.** Involuntary churn, and recovered revenue.

## Prerequisites

- A [verified sending domain](/settings/domain-authentication) and a [secret key](/integrations/api-keys).

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send payment events">
    Send `payment_failed` when a charge fails, and `payment_recovered` when it succeeds on retry. The recovery series runs between the two.

    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.onchainsuite.com/api/v1/events \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "event": "payment_failed",
        "contact": { "email": "alice@example.com" },
        "payload": { "reason": "card_expired", "amount_usd": 49.0 },
        "idempotencyKey": "dunning:inv-5521"
      }'
    ```

    Other languages follow the same shape, see the [API Reference](/api-reference).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the dunning series">
    Trigger on `payment_failed` and send a short sequence, each with a one-click link to fix the card:

    `trigger (payment_failed) → send email (Update your payment) → wait 2 days → send email (Still can't charge your card) → wait 3 days → send email (Last reminder before your account pauses)`

    Branch so anyone who sends `payment_recovered` exits immediately with a "you're all set."
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hand off if it still fails">
    If the series completes without recovery, tag the contact **Payment failed** so your team (or a downgrade flow) can take over.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    Both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Organize as you go.** Add `"tags": ["payment-failed"]` to the `payment_failed` event, or use an **Add tag** action, to flag at-risk accounts. See [Tags, lists, and segments](/integrations/tags-lists-segments).
</Note>

## Test

Send `payment_failed` with your own email, confirm the series fires on schedule. Send `payment_recovered` partway through and confirm it stops.

## Measure

- **Recovery rate** of failed payments.
- **Revenue saved** versus the pre-automation baseline.
- **Which email recovers the most** (usually the first), so you can tune timing.

## Variations

- **Route into a list, let the list run the flow.** Add the contact to a **Payment issues** list on the same `payment_failed` call and let that list's **List joined** trigger drive the follow-up, handy when several events should feed one flow for your failed payments. The list is created on first use.

  ```bash
  curl -X POST https://api.onchainsuite.com/api/v1/events \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -d '{ "event": "payment_failed", "contact": { "email": "alice@example.com" }, "addToList": "Payment issues" }'
  ```

  See [Triggering on a list join](/integrations/custom-events#triggering-on-a-list-join).
- **Branch on reason.** An expired card and an insufficient-funds failure may warrant different wording.
- **In-app banner.** Pair with an in-app prompt for users who log in during the dunning window.
