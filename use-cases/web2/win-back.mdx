---
title: "Win Back Quiet Subscribers"
description: "Re-engage subscribers who've stopped opening, then sunset the ones who stay quiet to protect your deliverability."
---

**Goal.** Spot subscribers who've gone quiet, send a re-engagement email to win them back, and gracefully stop emailing the ones who never respond, which keeps your sending reputation healthy.

**Who it's for.** Web2 teams working from email. The Web3 parallel, catching wallets that show on-chain exit signals, is [Win back dormant wallets](/use-cases/web3/win-back).

**Outcome to move.** Reactivation rate, and a cleaner, more engaged list.

## Prerequisites

- A [verified sending domain](/settings/domain-authentication).
- Enough history that "quiet" means something (at least a few sends).

## Build it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the quiet cohort">
    Build a segment of contacts who haven't opened or clicked in a while. The quickest way is a [SQL query](/intelligence/sql-query-editor) saved as a [segment](/intelligence/reports-and-segments#save-as-a-segment):

    ```sql
    SELECT email
    FROM contact_360
    WHERE last_engaged_at < now() - interval '60 days'
      AND email_unsubscribed = false
    ```

    Or build the same thing with rules in the [segment builder](/audience/segments) if you'd rather not write SQL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a re-engagement email">
    Create an automation that triggers when a contact **enters the quiet segment**, and sends one honest "we miss you" email, a reason to come back, not just a nudge.

    `trigger (entered Quiet segment) → send email (We miss you)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give them a beat, then decide">
    Wait a week, then branch on whether they re-engaged:

    `→ wait 7 days → branch (opened or clicked → tag Reactivated | else → tag Unengaged)`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sunset the ones who stay quiet">
    Contacts tagged **Unengaged** should drop out of your regular sends. Suppress them or move them to a low-frequency list. Continuing to email people who never open is the fastest way to hurt deliverability for everyone else.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate and publish.">
    As with any automation, both are required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Tagging.** Tag re-engaged contacts `reactivated` and persistently quiet ones `unengaged` with **Add tag** actions, so you can segment and suppress on them. See [Tags, lists, and segments](/integrations/tags-lists-segments).
</Note>

## Test

Add your own address to the quiet segment (or lower the window to a day for a test), and confirm the re-engagement email arrives and the branch routes you correctly when you do or don't open it.

## Measure

- **Reactivation rate**: share of the quiet cohort who opened or clicked the win-back.
- **Deliverability trend** on the [campaign report](/analytics/campaign-report), it should improve as you stop emailing the unengaged.
- **List health** in [Health and churn](/audience/health-and-churn).

## Variations

- **Offer an incentive.** A discount or a "here's what's new" can lift reactivation.
- **Preference center.** Let people choose fewer emails instead of leaving entirely.
- **Web3 parallel.** Watching for on-chain exit signals instead? See [Win back dormant wallets](/use-cases/web3/win-back).
