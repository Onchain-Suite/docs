## Core terms

### Workspace

The top-level container for your team, projects, and shared access.

### Project

The operational space where settings, audiences, campaigns, automations, and integrations live.

### Identity

A contact is one record that can be anchored by a **wallet**, an **email**, or your own **user id**. Any one is enough, and none requires the others, so a wallet-only contact and an email-only contact are both fully usable.

### Reachability

How a contact can be messaged: by **in-app** (for wallets signed in to your dApp), **email**, or both. Segments and sends can target by reachability.

### Audience

A set of contacts, identified by wallet, email, or both, selected for messaging, analysis, or lifecycle action.

### Segment

A reusable audience definition created from rules, queries, or saved logic.

### Campaign

A one-time or scheduled outbound message to a defined audience.

### Automation

A triggered workflow that listens for an event, checks conditions, and sends or branches actions.

### Play

A pre-built automation pattern for a common lifecycle moment: welcome, onboarding, purchase, or win-back, plus Web3 moments like first deposit or governance reminders.

### Double opt-in

A capture-form setting that holds a new email contact until they confirm by clicking a link, so only real, consenting addresses enter your audience.

### Publishable key

A client-safe key used for flows such as wallet registration or in-app challenge and verification.

### Secret key

A server-only key used for secure server-to-platform operations.
