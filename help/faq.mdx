## General

### Who is Onchain Suite for?

Growth, lifecycle, CRM, and operations teams working across Web3 and Web2: anyone who wants to act on wallet and customer behavior without stitching together a patchwork stack.

### Can I use it with just an email list (no wallets)?

Yes. Email-only works fully: identify people by email, send email campaigns and automations, run capture forms, and measure engagement. The wallet features (enrichment, on-chain triggers, holder analytics, in-app push) are optional extras for teams that have a wallet audience.

### Do I need a wallet, or an email, before I can get value?

Neither is required of the other. A connected wallet is a useful profile before any email is linked, and an email contact is fully usable with no wallet. Start from whichever you have.

### Do I need SQL to use the platform?

No. SQL is valuable for advanced cohort work, but many teams start with segments, reports, campaigns, and pre-built plays.

## Setup

### What should I configure first?

Start with project settings, sender verification, domain authentication, and the integrations needed for your first audience.

### What is the best first workflow?

A small internal campaign, a narrow audience segment, or one simple automation is usually the fastest way to validate your setup.

## Channels

### Is Onchain Suite email-only?

No. Email is one channel, but the platform is designed for multi-channel activation, including in-app and push for wallet audiences.
