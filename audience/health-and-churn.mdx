description: "Use engagement and risk signals to decide who needs follow-up, win-back, or a different channel."

Health and churn signals turn raw activity into prioritization: who's cooling off, who's worth a win-back, and who's healthy enough to leave alone.

## The signals

Enrichment and engagement data roll up into a few signals you can segment and query on:

- **Engagement score** (0 to 100), a weighted blend of recent on-chain activity, active days, holdings, and portfolio value. See [Wallet Enrichment](/audience/wallet-enrichment).
- **Churn risk**, derived from how recently a contact was active: `low` within 30 days, `medium` at 30 to 60, `high` beyond 60, plus `unknown` for wallets with no recorded activity.
- **Days since last activity**, the raw recency behind churn risk.

All three are columns on the [`contact_360`](/api/sql-schema#contact_360) view, so you can build any cohort from them in the [SQL editor](/intelligence/sql-query-editor).

## Questions they answer

- Which high-value wallets are cooling off and need follow-up?
- Which contacts open emails but have gone quiet on-chain?
- Which audience should get a campaign versus an automation branch?

```sql
-- Dormant whales worth a win-back
SELECT email, wallet_address, portfolio_value_usd, days_since_last_activity
FROM contact_360
WHERE portfolio_value_usd > 10000 AND churn_risk IN ('medium', 'high')
ORDER BY est_ltv DESC NULLS LAST
LIMIT 100
```

## Recommendation

Treat these as prioritization inputs alongside contract behavior, campaign response, and product context, not as isolated scores. A high churn risk on a wallet that just minted means something different from one that never engaged.
