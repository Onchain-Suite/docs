Audience is where raw wallet and contact data becomes people you can understand, group, and message. Every record is wallet-first: a contact can exist with just a wallet, with just an email, or with both, and everything you know about them attaches to that one record.

## Lists and tags

Contacts are organized with **lists** and **tags**. Every import lands in a list so its contacts are immediately findable, and tags add cross-cutting labels on top. Both can be renamed or deleted as your taxonomy changes.

## List hygiene

The audience keeps itself clean automatically:

- **Double opt-in** (on forms) holds a newly captured contact until they confirm by email, so only real, consenting addresses enter the audience.
- **Suppression** removes hard bounces and spam complaints, and they're never contacted again.
- **ONS+ verification** can clean addresses at import or capture time. See [Sender Verification](/settings/sender-verification).

## Why it matters

In Web3 a wallet alone isn't enough, and an email list alone isn't enough. Audience gives your team one operating model across both, so a campaign or automation can target "holds this NFT and opened the last email" without stitching systems together.
