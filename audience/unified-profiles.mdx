## Profile model

A profile is one record per person. It can include a wallet address, an email, your own user id, channel handles, tags, attributes, last activity, and engagement or health signals, whichever you have.

## Why this matters

Unified profiles make it easier to answer questions like:

- Can this person be reached in-app, by email, or both?
- What was the last meaningful product or on-chain action?
- Which users look healthy, cooling, or at risk?

## Where the context comes from

Product behavior comes in as [custom events](/integrations/custom-events). For contacts with a wallet, the portfolio value, activity, and holdings aren't entered by hand, they're fetched and derived by [wallet enrichment](/intelligence/wallet-enrichment) from the wallet's on-chain history. A Web2 contact with no wallet simply has no on-chain context, which is fine. Everything attaches to the same unified record.

## Recommendation

Treat profiles as operating records for lifecycle work, not just as static contact rows.
