Onchain Suite bills monthly. You either pick a plan with included allowances, or run on **Pay as you go** and pay for exactly what you use from a prepaid balance. There's no free tier and no annual discount, annual is simply twelve months up front.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Contacts | Email/mo | In-app/mo | Wallet-data credits | AI credits | Seats | Automations |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pay as you go** | $0 + usage | 1,000 | metered | metered | metered | metered | 2 | 3 |
| **Launch** | $39/mo | 2,500 | 50,000 | 25,000 | 1,000 | 500 | 2 | Unlimited |
| **Growth** | $349/mo | 25,000 | 250,000 | 250,000 | 10,000 | 8,000 | 4 | Unlimited |
| **Pro** | $1,622/mo | 75,000 | 750,000 | 1,000,000 | 25,000 | 16,000 | 7 | Unlimited |

Each plan carries a monthly email allowance and a monthly in-app allowance on top of one bundled price. A dedicated sending IP is provisioned automatically once an account sustains 100,000 sends a month; below that, sending runs on a managed warm pool.

<Note>
**Extra seats** are sold à la carte at **$10/month each**, above the seats your tier includes. A purchased seat raises only the seat limit, not your allowances, so it's the cheap way to add one teammate without moving up a plan. See [Team members](/settings/team-members#extra-seats).
</Note>

<Note>
Buying a contact count between two tiers is supported, and the price scales smoothly between them. You get the **capabilities** of the tier below your contact count and the **capacity** you paid for. For example, 11,000 contacts costs less than full Growth and carries Launch's features until you cross 25,000.
</Note>

## ONS+ list protection

ONS+ verifies email addresses at import or capture time, clearing typos, dead mailboxes, and spam traps before they hurt your sending reputation. It's available on every plan, metered at $10 per 1,000 addresses submitted, and a large first-stage check runs in-house at no charge, so most addresses never reach the paid stage.

Each paid plan includes a monthly ONS+ allowance equal to its contact cap, so verifying a list the size of your audience is covered rather than overage: 2,500 on Launch, 25,000 on Growth, and 75,000 on Pro. Buying more contact capacity raises the ONS+ allowance with it.

## Renewals and receipts

A subscription renews monthly. If a card is on file, it's charged automatically on the due date; otherwise you'll get a hosted invoice to pay. Reminder emails go out in the days before renewal, and a lapsed plan falls back to pay-as-you-go rather than cutting off.

Every paid invoice generates a branded PDF and emails a receipt to the workspace owner. Invoices are available in the billing area to view or download.

## Managing billing

Billing is owner-only. From the billing area you can change plan, buy contact capacity, top up a pay-as-you-go balance, manage payment methods (card or crypto), and read live usage against every allowance: contacts, messages, credits, seats, automations, and API keys.
