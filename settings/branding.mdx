Branding settings keep everything your users see, from emails to hosted pages, recognizably yours.

## What you can set

- **Brand colors**: a palette built around a primary color (entered as a hex value), used across the workspace's branded surfaces.
- **Email theme**: colors plus heading and body fonts applied in the email builder, so campaigns match your brand without per-email styling.
- **Default sender**: the verified sender identity new campaigns start from. See [Sender Verification](/settings/sender-verification).
- **Subdomain**: a workspace subdomain for hosted pages. Availability is checked as you type, and it's restricted to DNS-safe characters.

## Branding on transactional emails

Your logo and accent color don't just style campaigns, they carry into the **transactional** emails the platform sends on your behalf: teammate invites, email verification, password resets, and double opt-in [capture form](/audience/capture-forms) confirmations. Each renders in a consistent, email-client-safe layout:

- **Your logo** sits at the top of the card when one is set; without a logo, your workspace name stands in as text.
- **Your accent color** drives the header rule and the call-to-action button. Only valid hex colors (`#rgb` or `#rrggbb`) are used; anything else falls back to the platform default.
- Every one of these emails carries a **"Powered by OnchainSuite"** line in the footer, alongside your own "Sent by \<your brand\>". "OnchainSuite" is a link to [onchainsuite.com](https://onchainsuite.com).

<Note>
Double opt-in [capture form](/audience/capture-forms) confirmation emails use this same branded layout, your logo and accent, and are sent from your **verified sender** so they route on your own sending domain. The confirmation link opens a hosted confirmation page that carries the same branding. This keeps confirmations on the same rails, and the same look, as the rest of your mail.
</Note>

Set the logo and accent once and every transactional surface inherits them, there's nothing to configure per email.

## Recommendations

- Match branding to the live product users already recognize.
- Set the default sender and email theme once so every new campaign inherits them.
- Keep naming consistent across sender identities and templates.
- Review every visible brand element before your first production send.
