Invite teammates by email and give each one a role. Roles control what they can change; the number of seats is set by your plan.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and transferring ownership. There's always at least one. |
| **Admin** | Manage settings, domains, API keys, team members, and campaigns. Not billing. |
| **Editor** | Create and run campaigns, automations, and audiences. No settings or team management. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only: dashboards, analytics, and reporting. |

Managing members (inviting, changing roles, removing) is limited to Owners and Admins. The last Owner can't be removed or downgraded, so a workspace always has one.

## Seats

Each plan includes a fixed number of seats, counting the Owner:

| Plan | Seats |
| --- | --- |
| Pay as you go | 2 |
| Launch | 2 |
| Growth | 4 |
| Pro | 7 |
| Scale | Custom |

### Extra seats

Need one more person than your tier includes? You don't have to jump a whole plan for it. **Additional seats are available at $10/month each**, on top of the seats your plan already bundles. Buying a seat raises only your seat limit, it doesn't change your contact, message, or credit allowances (those come with the plan), and it never lowers you below the seats your tier includes.

See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing).

## Inviting

Send an invite from the team settings; the teammate accepts via an emailed link. Invites are rate-limited to a handful per minute, and you can resend or cancel a pending one.

## Good practices

- Grant the least access each person needs: Viewer for stakeholders who only read, Editor for people running campaigns.
- Reserve Admin for people who manage domains, keys, and settings.
- Review access after team changes, and keep ownership of senders, domains, and integrations clear.
