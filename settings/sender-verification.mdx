---
title: "Sender Verification"
description: "Verify the from-address your campaigns send from, and keep your list clean so it stays deliverable."
---

Your campaigns send from a verified sender identity — the name and address recipients see. Verifying it is what lets mailbox providers trust the mail, and it's required before a campaign can go out.

## Verifying a sender

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate the domain first">
    A sender address lives on a domain. Authenticate that domain — SPF, DKIM, DMARC — before adding senders on it. See [Domain Authentication](/settings/domain-authentication).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the sender">
    Set the from-name and from-address on the verified domain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and test">
    Once it's available, send a test campaign and confirm it lands as expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Keeping the list deliverable

A verified sender only stays deliverable if you send to real, engaged addresses. Two features help:

- **ONS+ verification** cleans addresses — typos, dead mailboxes, spam traps — at import or capture time. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing) for how it's metered. You can also run a one-off verification pass over an existing list.
- **Double opt-in** (on forms) holds a newly captured contact until they click a confirmation link, so only real, consenting addresses ever enter your audience.

Hard bounces and spam complaints are added to a suppression list automatically and never contacted again.

## Recommendations

- Use a from-name recipients recognize instantly.
- Keep sender identities specific to a project or brand.
- Don't switch sender identity between related campaigns without a clear reason — consistency builds recipient trust.
- Before going live, confirm the sender is verified, its domain is authenticated, and a test send lands.
