Authenticating your domain proves Onchain Suite is allowed to send on its behalf, which is what gets your campaigns past spam filtering and into the inbox. It's a one-time DNS setup per domain.

## Setup flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Register the domain you'll send from in domain settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the DNS records">
    The setup shows the exact records to add — see [DNS Records](/settings/dns-records) for what each one is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them at your DNS provider">
    Enter them manually, or use the automatic option if your DNS provider is connected — it adds them for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Re-check after the records propagate. Verification passes once every record resolves correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What gets checked

A complete setup has four kinds of record:

- **Ownership** — a TXT record proving you control the domain.
- **SPF** — a TXT record (`v=spf1 …`) listing who may send for the domain.
- **DKIM** — **two CNAME records** that let outgoing mail be cryptographically signed.
- **DMARC** — a TXT record telling receivers what to do with mail that fails the checks.

## Handling conflicts

The setup inspects your existing DNS and tells you exactly what to change rather than leaving you to guess:

- **An existing SPF record is merged, not replaced.** Two separate `v=spf1` records is an invalid setup, so if you already have one, you add the Onchain Suite include to it rather than adding a second record.
- **A clashing DKIM selector** — if the CNAME host is already taken — is flagged so you can clear it.
- **An existing DMARC record** is left as-is; you don't need a second one.

You'll also see advisories for weak existing policies, like an SPF ending in `~all` or a DMARC at `p=none`.

## Troubleshooting

- Add records to the correct host — a trailing domain doubled onto the host is the most common mistake.
- Watch for stray quotes or characters copied around a value.
- Give DNS time to propagate before re-checking.
- If SPF won't verify, confirm you have a single merged `v=spf1` record, not two.
