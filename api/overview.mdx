Onchain Suite drops into your dApp. Most teams start in the frontend, wiring up wallet-based notifications, then send product events and read data from their backend as they grow.

The in-app SDK authenticates users by **wallet signature**, so you can reach someone who has never given you an email. Nothing about it requires a backend: a publishable key and the SDK are enough to start.

## How keys work

| Key | Where it lives | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Publishable (`pk_*`) | Browser, safe to ship | The in-app SDK's wallet auth |
| Secret (`sk_*`) | Server only | Server-to-server API calls |

See [API Keys](/integrations/api-keys) for the full model, including scopes and rotation.

## The shape of an integration

Most implementations follow the same arc:

1. **Add the SDK** to your dApp so wallets can receive notifications.
2. **Send events** from your product, either from the browser through a form or from your backend with a secret key.
3. **Let the platform work.** Campaigns, audiences, and automations operate on the normalized result, and reach users on whichever channel they're on.
