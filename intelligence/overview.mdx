Intelligence is where your on-chain and off-chain data meet, so you can answer questions about your audience and turn the answers into audiences you can message. It handles questions like:

- Which of my holders never open my emails?
- Which high-value wallets have gone quiet this month?
- Which cohort responded best to the last campaign?
- Which segment is worth activating next?

There are four ways in, and they all read from the same data.

## Start with a question

The fastest path is [Agentic Chat](/intelligence/agentic-chat). Ask something like "which of my holders never open my emails," and the assistant reasons across your on-chain and email data, answers in plain English, and offers to save the result as a segment. It's the front door to everything else here.

## The data behind every answer

Every on-chain answer, in chat or SQL, reads from **enriched wallet data**: each wallet's portfolio value, recent activity, NFT holdings, and derived engagement and value scores. The "Data enriched · N wallets" status at the top of Intelligence shows how many of your wallets are enriched and how fresh that data is. Answers are only as complete as that set, so [enrich more wallets](/audience/wallet-enrichment) or refresh before a big analysis.

## When to reach for SQL

Chat handles most questions. Reach for the [SQL editor](/intelligence/sql-query-editor) when you need an exact, repeatable cohort in one precise definition, or want to save a query and re-run it for free. Prefer to describe it in words? [Generate a draft query](/intelligence/generate-sql) from a plain-language prompt, then refine it.

## Turn answers into audiences

However you get there, chat, a [starter query](/intelligence/starter-queries), or your own SQL, the output becomes a [segment](/intelligence/segments-from-queries): a saved, reusable audience you drop into a campaign or automation. That's the whole point of Intelligence, not just to learn something, but to act on it.
