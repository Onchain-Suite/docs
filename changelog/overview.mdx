What's shipped recently, newest first. For deeper how-to, follow the links into the guides.

<Update label="October 2026" description="Web3 + Web2, Use Cases, and a new docs layout">
  **Onchain Suite now serves Web2 teams too.** You can run the whole product on email alone, no wallets required, alongside the wallet-native Web3 features.

  - **Use Cases** is a new section of end-to-end playbooks, split into [Web3](/use-cases/web3/welcome-wallet) and [Web2](/use-cases/web2/welcome-email) tracks, with copy-paste code in five languages.
  - The docs are reorganized into **User Guides** (for marketers) and **Developer Guides** (for developers), plus Use Cases, API Reference, and this Changelog.
</Update>

<Update label="September 2026" description="Intelligence, led by Agentic Chat">
  - **[Agentic Chat](/intelligence/agentic-chat)** answers questions about your on-chain and email audience in plain English, and can build a segment for you.
  - **[Reports and Segments](/intelligence/reports-and-segments)** brings generating a query, saving a report, and saving a segment into one place.
  - **[Wallet enrichment](/intelligence/wallet-enrichment)** now lives in Intelligence as the data behind every answer.
</Update>

<Update label="August 2026" description="Automation and Forms">
  - The automation builder gained **A/B split**, **remove tag**, and **add / remove from list**, for 11 actions and a fuller trigger catalog.
  - A new **[Forms](/integrations/forms)** guide covers embedding a capture form, submitting to it, and verifying a wallet.
</Update>
