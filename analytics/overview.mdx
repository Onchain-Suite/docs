<Note>
**Live.** These reports are shipped and available in your workspace today. A few individual metrics aren't wired up yet and show as `0` (for example push view rate and conversions); each is called out on its own page.
</Note>

Analytics reports turn the data already in your workspace, on-chain holdings, contacts, delivery events, and automation runs, into the cards you see on the analytics dashboard. Every report is read-only: nothing here sends a message, changes your data, or spends credits.

## Who can see them

Analytics lives in your dashboard and always shows the workspace you're currently in. Because the reports only read data and never change anything, **every role can open them, including Viewer**. There's nothing to set up: as soon as you have contacts, holders, or campaigns, the cards fill in.

<Note>
Need this data in your own tools instead of the dashboard? The same numbers are queryable through the [SQL editor](/intelligence/sql-query-editor), and your backend can pull data with the [server-to-server API](/integrations/api-keys).
</Note>
