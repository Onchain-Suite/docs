---
title: "Engagement by Segment"
description: "Per-segment email sent, open, and click rates."
---

Engagement by segment compares how each of your [segments](/audience/segments) responds to email, one row per segment, with sent volume and open and click rates. It's the fastest way to see which audiences are worth more of your sending.

## What it shows

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Segment | The audience, by name. |
| Sent | How many emails were delivered to that segment. |
| Open rate | Opens as a share of emails sent. |
| Click rate | Clicks as a share of emails sent. |
| Push view rate | Reserved for in-app view rate. **Shows `0` today**, not yet wired up. |

Rates are worked out per segment from your delivery events and each segment's membership.

## Powers

The **Engagement by segment** table on the analytics dashboard.
