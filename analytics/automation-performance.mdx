---
title: "Automation Performance"
description: "Per-flow enrolments, completion rate, and downstream engagement."
---

Automation performance reports on each [automation](/automation/overview): how many contacts entered, how many completed, and how the emails it sent performed. One row per automation, newest first, up to the last 200.

## What it shows

| Column | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Automation | The flow and its current status. |
| Entered | How many contacts were enrolled into the flow. |
| Completed | How many of them reached the end. |
| Completion rate | Completed as a share of entered. |
| Sent | Emails delivered across the flow's runs. |
| Open rate / Click rate | Opens and clicks as a share of emails sent. |
| Conversions | Reserved for goal tracking. **Shows `0` today**, not yet populated. |

## Powers

The **Automations** performance table on the analytics dashboard.
