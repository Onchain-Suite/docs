---
title: "Holder Distribution"
description: "How token holdings spread across wallets, the largest holders, and concentration metrics."
---

Holder distribution buckets your holders by balance, surfaces the largest wallets, and measures how concentrated ownership is. It reads from the on-chain holdings that [wallet sync](/audience/wallet-enrichment#syncing-holders) discovers, so it's empty until you've synced holders.

## What you can filter

- **By contract.** Focus on a single token, or include every holding in your workspace.
- **Top holders shown.** Choose how many of the largest wallets to list, up to 200.

## What it shows

| On the card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Balance buckets | Holders grouped into fixed ranges (`0–1`, `1–10`, `10–100`, `100–1K`, `1K–10K`, `10K+`), with how many wallets fall in each band and that band's share of supply. |
| Top holders | The largest wallets by balance, each with the percentage of supply it holds. |
| Concentration | How much of the supply the ten largest wallets hold, plus a standard concentration score, where a higher number means ownership sits in fewer wallets. |
| Totals | The number of distinct holding wallets and the total supply in view. |

## Powers

The **Holder distribution** card on the analytics dashboard: the bucketed bar chart, the top-holders table, and the concentration read-out.
